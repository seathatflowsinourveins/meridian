RULE (verification fact sheet, "Measurement protocol", verbatim)
- With-arm valid only if --debug-file shows 'Loaded 1 LSP server(s) from plugin: pyright-lsp', 'Starting LSP server instance: pyright' and at least one 'LSP Diagnostics: Delivering'.
- Adopt only if the method's rule holds (2 of 3 vs at most 1, or equal and at least 25% fewer median tokens) and the venv binary and setup.sh change are in place.

THE SAME CONDITIONS IN THE ASSESSMENT (measurement_protocol.adopt_if, and the last two sentences of runs_and_metrics, verbatim)
Decided before running: adopt only if all with-arm runs are valid and either (a) with-arm success in at least 2 of 3 runs while the without-arm succeeds in at most 1 of 3, or (b) equal success counts and median total tokens at least 25% lower in the with-arm. The always-on cost (about 3 tokens per request) and every diagnostics injection are already inside the measured totals. Also required: pyright-langserver comes from the worktree's .venv (pyright 1.1.414 = uv.lock = CI), and Orca can start Claude Code with that PATH without a global pyright. Otherwise not adopted.
Validity of a with-arm run: the debug log contains 'Loaded 1 LSP server(s) from plugin: pyright-lsp' and 'Starting LSP server instance: pyright', and at least one delivery. An invalid run is repeated, not counted.

DEFAULT RULE (ab-claude-code.md as used, verbatim)
Adopt only if the arm with the addition passes in at least 2 of 3 runs and the arm without in at most 1; or both pass in at least 2 of 3 and the arm with the addition uses at least 25% fewer median tokens; and in either case the always-on delta is at most 2,000 tokens unless the first condition holds.

TIE RULE (ab-tasks.md as used, verbatim)
3 runs per arm, one at a time, interleaved (a1, b1, b2, a2, a3, b3), with the same binary, model, effort and flags in both arms. If the arms differ by one success, or tie with 2 each, run 2 more per arm.
