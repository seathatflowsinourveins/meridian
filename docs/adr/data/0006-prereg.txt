RULE (measure.json, adoption_rule, verbatim)
Method default, stated before any run. Adopt only if the arm with the addition passes at least 2 of 3 runs and the arm without passes at most 1 of 3, or both arms pass at least 2 of 3 and the with arm uses at least 25% fewer median tokens (sum over modelUsage of input, cache-creation, cache-read and output; total_cost_usd is reported only). In either case the always-on delta must be at most 2,000 tokens unless the first condition holds, and there must be zero containment violations; a with-arm run that breaks containment is void and counts against the addition. If the arms differ by one success or tie at 2 each, run 2 more per arm.

DEFAULT RULE (ab-claude-code.md, verbatim)
Adopt only if the arm with the addition passes in at least 2 of 3 runs and the arm without in at most 1; or both pass in at least 2 of 3 and the arm with the addition uses at least 25% fewer median tokens; and in either case the always-on delta is at most 2,000 tokens unless the first condition holds.
